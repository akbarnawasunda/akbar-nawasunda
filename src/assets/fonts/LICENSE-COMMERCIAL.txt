COMMERCIAL LICENCES — HELD BY THE SITE OWNER, NOT REDISTRIBUTED HERE
====================================================================

The following five typefaces are commercial fonts purchased by the site owner
(Akbar Nawasunda) and licensed for use on this website. The licence documents
themselves are held by the owner off-repository; only the webfont binaries the
licence permits to be self-hosted are committed here, in this folder:

  Excess V Straight.woff2     display role  (--font-display)
  Psychlone Straight.woff2    heading role  (--font-heading)
  Relidux.woff2               body role     (--font-body)
  Impact Label SWL.woff2      label role    (--font-label)
  Marseloup.woff2             accent role   (--font-accent)

Each binary has been subset to the Latin ranges the site sets (basic Latin,
Latin-1 punctuation, general punctuation, arrows) and re-packed as WOFF2 with
FontTools. Subsetting for self-hosting is a standard webfont practice; glyph
metrics and the family name are untouched, so what renders is the licensed
face exactly as drawn.

Do not copy these five files into other projects. They are not open-licensed.

The remaining file in this folder, anton-latin-400.woff2, is a different case:
Anton is open-licensed (SIL OFL 1.1) and its licence text ships beside it in
LICENSE-anton.txt. It is used only by scripts/generate-placeholder-covers.mjs
to rasterise placeholder cover art — it is not served to any browser as page
type.
